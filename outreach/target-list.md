## How to find coordinator emails

1. Go to the department page for the programme.
2. Look for "Study Advisor", "Programme Coordinator", "Academic Coordinator", or "Student Affairs".
3. Most pages list names + direct emails. If not, try: `firstname.lastname@university.edu`
4. LinkedIn search: `"programme coordinator" "MSc Computer Science" "TU Munich"` also works.

Log everything in the tracking spreadsheet.
