## The finding that reframes the campaign

Batch 4 split universities by whether an incumbent tool exists. Batch 5 shows the sharper question is
**what layer the tool validates at** — and nearly every one validates the wrong layer.

| University | Tool | What it actually checks | The gap sold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warsaw / all Poland | USOS | Registration limits, tury, żetony, dedykacje; then arithmetic **after** the session on already-graded courses | Never checks whether the chosen set can satisfy the degree. MIMUW tells students so outright. |
| UPC | e-Secretaria | Four requisite types on one semester's *potencial de matrícula* | Never the whole plan against graduation rules |
| TCD | my.tcd.ie OME | 30 ECTS/semester, 60/year, co-requisites | Composition and history-dependent rules (e.g. "5 ECTS of ML/AI by end of year 5") no per-semester counter can evaluate |

| Paris-Saclay | *contrat pédagogique* | Validated by a human; UE choice collected on a Word/PDF sheet — and Google Forms for the AI track | The whole thing is manual, and legally mandated by 15 November |
| IST Lisbon | **Fénix** | Encodes curricular rules as first-class objects; enforces ECTS caps, group scoping, coordinator approval | **None worth selling — see below** |

**"Counts a sum, not a structure"** is the line that works across most of this batch.

## Incumbent tooling / approval process

**Short version: tooling exists for applying and for administrative registration. The UE-choice step itself is documented, by the university, as something each programme's pedagogical secretariat handles by hand — and the validation is an explicit human act by the responsable de formation.**

### 1. The "contrat pédagogique" is real, formal, and named in the regulations

From the *Règlement des études / MCC / Charte des examens — Master*, Université Paris-Saclay (official PDF, §2 "Contrat pédagogique individuel"):

> « Un contrat pédagogique nominatif annuel ou semestriel, établi par l'établissement référent de l'EF, définit la liste des UE que l'étudiant s'engage à suivre durant l'année et pour lesquelles il sera évalué. L'établissement référent s'assure que le contrat pédagogique, **préalablement validé par le responsable de l'élément de formation**, est signé par l'étudiant. Le contrat pédagogique annuel ou du 1er semestre est signé **au plus tard le 15 novembre** de l'année universitaire d'inscription. Le contrat pédagogique du second semestre est signé **au plus tard le 15 février**. »

> « Les modifications de contrat pédagogique au cours de l'année ne peuvent intégrer des changements d'UEs pour lesquelles les examens ou validations ont eu lieu. **Les modifications sont validées nécessairement par le responsable de la formation.** »

Same document, §1.2, on what every élément de formation must publish:
> « La liste des unités d'enseignement (UE), avec le nombre de crédits ECTS correspondant à chacune d'entre elles, **Les contraintes de choix (UE optionnelle ou obligatoire)**, Les règles de validation à l'année : notes seuil à l'UE, au BCC éventuellement »

Same document, §1.4, on substitution:
> « Lorsque les parcours de master permettent de substituer une UE de la maquette par une UE d'une autre formation (exemples : cursus ingénieur, médecine ou pharmacie), les règles de substitution devront être explicites et devront être mentionnées dans un contrat pédagogique. »

And — this is the strongest single line for the pitch — the jury is required to police it twice a year:
> « Les jurys d'EF … doivent assurer les missions suivantes : validation des résultats à l'UE et au bloc, **respect du contrat pédagogique pour chaque étudiant** … une réunion obligatoire à mi-année (**il s'agira ici de faire un point sur le contrat pédagogique de chaque étudiant**) »

Source: https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/sites/default/files/2024-05/reglement_des_etudes_mcc_charte_des_examens_-_master.pdf

## Corrections to the brief

Four things in the brief are wrong or imprecise. Fixing them matters because getting the vocabulary wrong is what makes a French coordinator delete the email.

1. **"Master IA" is not a mention. It is a *parcours*.** "Artificial Intelligence [AI]" is a track *inside* the Master mention Informatique. It even says so on its own site: *"The AI master is a 'parcours' (track) of the Université Paris-Saclay Computer Science (Informatique) master."* Separately there is an **M1/M2 Mathématiques et Intelligence Artificielle** which sits in the mention *Mathématiques et applications* — a different mention, run jointly with CentraleSupélec. Calling it "Master IA" to a coordinator signals you haven't read their page. (Sources: https://ai-master.lisn.upsaclay.fr/ , https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/formation/master/mathematiques-et-applications/m1-mathematiques-et-intelligence-artificielle)

2. **There is no "Master Électronique".** The mention is **« Électronique, énergie électrique, automatique »**, universally abbreviated **E3A** (occasionally EEA). It is a multi-site mention: M1 runs at Orsay-Gif, Évry, Versailles (UVSQ) and via the *Voie André Ampère* at ENS Paris-Saclay, with ~7 M1 and ~16 M2 parcours, under the Graduate School *Sciences de l'ingénierie et des systèmes*. Source: https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/formation/master/electronique-energie-electrique-automatique

3. **eCandidat is not used here.** Paris-Saclay runs its own platform, **Inception**, alongside the national **Mon Master**. eCandidat is a different (Sup'-Ensemble/AMUE) product used by other French universities. Saying "eCandidat" in an email to Paris-Saclay would be a visible tell. Source: https://inception.universite-paris-saclay.fr/fr/

4. **"The many parcours" is an understatement, and it's split across two websites that disagree.** The central Paris-Saclay page for the mention Informatique lists **12 M1 parcours and 16 M2 parcours**. The Orsay Département d'Informatique site lists **6**. Both are correct — the Orsay site covers only the Faculté des Sciences d'Orsay tracks (AI, IoT, DS, HCI, QDCS, MPRI); the rest are operated by other Paris-Saclay components (Évry, UVSQ, CentraleSupélec, ENS). If you write "your six tracks" to a central contact, or "your sixteen" to Orsay, you're wrong either way. Name the specific parcours instead. Sources: https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/formation/master/informatique and https://master-info-orsay.lri.fr/

**One thing the brief got right and should be kept verbatim:** "UE obligatoires / UE au choix". The regulation's own wording is « Les contraintes de choix (UE optionnelle ou obligatoire) » — close enough that using it reads as native.

## Hook

**The 15 November deadline, and the arithmetic that has to be right before it.**

Paris-Saclay writes the contrat pédagogique into its regulations with a hard date, a named validator (the responsable de formation), and a mid-year jury meeting whose stated job is to review *every* student's contract. Meanwhile the UE-choice step that produces that contract is a Word file or a Google Form. StudyPlanner sits in that one gap: the student checks their own plan before it reaches the coordinator's desk.

Secondary hook, per-parcours and very concrete — the ECTS arithmetic is genuinely fiddly:
- **QDCS**: 60 ECTS/year where **each course is worth 2.5 ECTS** — roughly 24 units a year to assemble, plus 7 soft-skills UEs (4 in M1, 3 in M2) and free choice from other parcours.
- **AI**: M1 = Bloc 1 of 16 UEs × 3 ECTS = 48 ECTS, plus Bloc 2 = TER (6 ECTS, obligatory) + 6 ECTS = 12; M2 = 9 UEs propres + 1 soft skill = 30 ECTS, plus a 30-ECTS internship. Several Bloc-1 slots are explicitly *"class from other track [DKAI] / [DIPAQ] / [HCI]"*.
- **Mention-wide**: the Orsay site's stated philosophy is cross-parcours choice — *"la possibilité de suivre des UEs libres issues d'autres parcours"*. That is precisely the case where a plan silently drifts out of compliance.

**Incumbent tooling / approval process:**

**Verdict: tooling exists AND it validates — at enrolment time, per semester. It does not validate a multi-semester plan against graduation requirements. Position StudyPlanner strictly as a pre-enrolment planning layer, never as a replacement.**

What is publicly documented:

1. **e-Secretaria (UPC-wide) enforces requisites at enrolment.** FIB's own Academic Regulations for Bachelor's and Master's Degrees 2026-2027 (Standing Committee Decision CP.FIB/2026/05/04), §2.5 "Requisites between subjects", defines **four** requisite types that gate enrolment: *prerequisite* (A must be passed before B can be enrolled), *corequisite* (A passed or enrolled), *precorequisite* (A enrolled at least one semester earlier; chained precorequisites collapse to "passed"), and *orequisite* (A **or** B passed before C). It also encodes a graduation-year exemption: if a student can finish this year and only a prerequisite blocks it, "the prerequisite in question is considered a corequisite."
   Source: https://www.fib.upc.edu/sites/fib/files/2026-07/normativa-academica-fib-2026-2027-en.pdf
2. **The "potencial de matrícula" in e-Secretaria is pre-filtered per student.** FIB's enrolment page tells students to check their *potencial* in e-Secretaria before enrolling.
   Source: https://www.fib.upc.edu/ca/estudis/secretaria/tramits/matricula-de-masters
3. **FIB publicly admits the potential is scoped to your specialisation, and going outside it is a manual request.** Verbatim from the master's enrolment page:
   > "IMPORTANT: Si vols matricular assignatures d'una altra especialitat, ho hauràs de sol·licitar durant el període de modificació de matrícula mitjançant una instància a l'e-Secretaria."
   And from the "Afegir assignatura al potencial de la matrícula" procedure:
   > "Pel que fa als estudis de màster, les assignatures que no formen part del propi pla d'estudis o especialitat s'han de sol·licitar durant el període establert de Modificació de Matrícula i Canvi de Grup."
   Sources: https://www.fib.upc.edu/ca/estudis/secretaria/tramits/matricula-de-masters and https://www.fib.upc.edu/ca/estudis/secretaria/tramits/afegir-assignatura-al-potencial-de-la-matricula
4. **FIB does build its own systems — the brief was right about that.** Modification of enrolment and group changes are done in **Racó** (FIB's own intranet), *not* e-Secretaria; only out-of-curriculum subjects (i.e. from another master's programme) route through e-Secretaria. Verbatim: "Les sol·licituds de Modificació de matrícula i de Canvi de Grup es fan a través del Racó."
   Source: https://www.fib.upc.edu/ca/estudis/secretaria/tramits/modificacio-de-matricula-i-canvi-de-grup
5. **Volume/load rules are also machine-enforced:** full-time max 36 ECTS/semester, part-time max 36 ECTS/year (18/semester); master's students must reach a minimum of 15 ECTS/year; students may over-enrol optional credits by up to 10% of a master's credits. (Regs §2, §2.4.)

**The documented gap — what nothing at UPC publicly does:**
Every mechanism above answers *"may I enrol in X next semester?"*. Nothing published answers *"will the four semesters I am sketching actually satisfy my especialitat and add up to the degree?"* Three concrete pieces of public evidence that this forward-looking check is currently a human/prose exercise:

- **ETSETB publishes MET co-enrolment constraints as prose on a web page**, not as a checkable artefact. Verbatim from the MET curriculum page: "AFOC requires to simultaneously enrol TSYS. / AMC requires to simultaneously enrol TSYS. / QSN requires to simultaneously enrol CN and OVNET. / WAN requires to simultaneously enrol CN." Plus a pure *completion* rule that no enrolment-time filter can catch: "the student must have passed 3 of the same intensification **before finishing the master**."
  Source: https://telecos.upc.edu/en/study-programs/masters/masters-degree-in-telecommunications-engineering-met/curriculum
- **MIRI's specialisation ECTS requirement has a per-specialisation exception buried in a PDF.** Regs §8: "MIRI: 30 common compulsory ECTS and 24 compulsory specialisation ECTS **or 18 ECTS in the case of the HPC specialisation**."

These subjects do not extend the master, they use elective credits." A student's remaining elective budget therefore depends on an assignment made by a committee — a cross-semester arithmetic question, not an enrolment-time one.

Approval is per-semester enrolment (automatic, via the potential) plus an *instància* to the **Àrea de Gestió Acadèmica (Secretaria)** for out-of-specialisation subjects, resolved in the modification window. For mobility, §8.1 of the regs says the **coordinator of the specialisation** must approve complementary specialisation credits in the learning agreement, and the learning agreement is signed by the student, the **vice-dean for International Relations**, and the receiving institution's mobility officer. # 3. TU Berlin

Faculty IV — Elektrotechnik und Informatik (EECS). Researched 2026-08-27.

---

**Incumbent tooling / approval process:**

TU Berlin runs **MOSES** (by innoCampus), which contains the **MTS (Modultransfersystem / Modulverzeichnis)**, the VVZ (course catalogue), and the semester planning processes (KPL exam scheduling, VPL course planning, TPL tutorial allocation). Grades live in **QISPOS**; ISIS is the Moodle LMS and is not involved in planning. A separate SAP-based **SLM (Student Lifecycle Management)** system exists but currently handles only Physik (B.Sc./M.Sc.) and Historische Urbanistik (M.A.) via TUport.

The important part: **MTS already models the rules but does not check a student's plan against them.**

- MTS explicitly stores the rules: *"Um die Wahlmöglichkeiten der Module abzubilden, werden die Module in Studiengangsbereichen organisiert und mit den entsprechenden Wahlregeln versehen. Da sich mit der Zeit die Module innerhalb eines Studiengangs ändern können, werden die Studiengangsbereiche in einer semesterabhängigen Modulliste gespeichert."* — <https://moseskonto.tu-berlin.de/moses/modultransfersystem/studiengaenge/index.html>
- But the student-facing side is read-only browsing: *"Studierende haben die Möglichkeit sich die bislang freigegebenen Module anzusehen."* MTS is also described by TU Berlin itself as *"das übergangsweise Angebot einer zentralen Moduldatenbank"* whose data *"werden dem zukünftigen SLM-System als Grundlage dienen."* — <https://moseskonto.tu-berlin.de/moses/modultransfersystem/index.html>
- The only validation that fires anywhere is at **per-module exam registration**, and it is single-module, not plan-level. TU Berlin's own student tutorial shows the checks are: does the registration window happen to be open (green vs. red), and has an exam been created for that module. Module selection is a free-text search over module titles/numbers. — *Moses: Modulprüfungsanmeldung im MTS – Für Studierende*, V.230324, <https://www.static.tu.berlin/fileadmin/www/10004059/innoc_moses_dokumente/moses_tutorial_-_Modulpru__fungsanmeldung_fu__r_Studierende_V230324.pdf>
- **The strongest documented gap** is step 5b of that same tutorial. The student picks, per module, which **Anrechnungsbereich** the module should count towards — and TU Berlin warns: *"Wenn Sie den Anrechnungsbereich später ändern möchten, wenden Sie sich bitte direkt an die Modul-/Prüfungsverantwortlichen und fragen dort nach, ob dies ggf. noch möglich ist."* So the area allocation is committed module-by-module, over four semesters, with no view of the aggregate, and undoing a wrong allocation is discretionary. That is exactly the shape of problem a plan-level validator solves.

**Approval process — differs per programme, which is the headline finding:**

| Programme | Individual study plan needs Prüfungsausschuss approval? |
|---|---|
| M.Sc. Computer Science (Informatik) | **No.** No approval step exists anywhere in the StuPO. |
| M.Sc. Computer Engineering | **No** (same StuPO template, §5(1)). |
| M.Sc. ICT Innovation | **Yes, for deviations** — see quote below. |

---

**Corrections to the brief:**

Four things in the brief are wrong for TU Berlin. The first is the big one.

**1. "BEFORE the plan is submitted to the Prüfungsausschuss for approval" is false for M.Sc. Informatik / Computer Science.** There is no such submission and no such approval. The StuPO says the opposite: *"§5 (1) Die Studierenden haben das Recht, ihren Studienablauf individuell zu gestalten. Sie sind jedoch verpflichtet, die Vorgaben dieser Studien- und Prüfungsordnung einzuhalten. Die Abfolge von Modulen wird durch den exemplarischen Studienverlaufsplan als Anlage 2 dieser Ordnung empfohlen."* I searched the full consolidated text for *Zustimmung*, *Genehmigung*, *Studienplan* — zero hits for an approval requirement. The only "Studienplan" mention is the recommendation to draft one **before going abroad** and clear recognition with the module owners or the Prüfungsausschuss (§5(6)). I also checked the CS examination board's downloadable forms: there are exactly four (Nachteilsausgleich DE/EN, Formloser Antrag, three thesis-extension forms) — **no individual-study-plan form exists**. Sources: consolidated StuPO <https://www.static.tu.berlin/fileadmin/www/10000040/1_Studium_Lehre/1_Studienangebot/2_Masterstudiengaenge/2_M_CS/Lesefassung_MSc_ComputerScience_DE.pdf>; forms list on <https://www.tu.berlin/en/eecs/academics-teaching/examination-boards/computer-science>.

So for Informatik the pitch cannot be "check it before the Prüfungsausschuss sees it." The honest framing is: **nobody checks it until the end.** The student self-assembles across four semesters, commits an Anrechnungsbereich per module at registration, and the arithmetic only gets reconciled at graduation. That is a stronger pitch, not a weaker one — but it has to be said correctly or the recipient will spot the error immediately.

**2. ICT Innovation is the one programme where the brief's framing IS literally correct.** M.Sc. ICT Innovation StuPO, §5(3): *"Davon abweichende Modulkombinationen, inklusive des Austauschs von Pflichtmodulen, können auf Antrag der/des Studierenden vom Prüfungsausschuss genehmigt werden."* Deviating module combinations, including swapping compulsory modules, require an application and board approval. Source: <https://www.static.tu.berlin/fileadmin/www/10000000/Studiengaenge/StuPOs/Fakultaet_IV/ICTInnovation_M.Sc._2016.pdf> (current through 6. Änderungssatzung, AMBl 27/2021).

**3. "Pflicht vs Wahlpflicht vs Wahl" does not describe M.Sc. Informatik.** That programme has **zero Pflichtmodule**. 120 LP = 90 LP modules + 30 LP thesis, and the 90 splits as Wahlpflicht 60–66 LP + Wahlbereich 24–30 LP. There is nothing compulsory. The real structure is **six Studiengebiete**, of which one must carry 30–42 LP and the rest 18–36 LP:

- Data and Software Engineering
- Eingebettete Systeme und Rechnerarchitekturen / Embedded Systems and Computer Architectures
- Grundlagen der Informatik / Foundations of Computing
- Kognitive Systeme / Cognitive Systems
- Medientechnik und Mensch-Maschine-Interaktion / Digital Media and HCI
- Verteilte Systeme und Netze / Distributed Systems and Networks

Plus a seventh, **Informationssysteme / Information Systems**, added by the 2. Änderungssatzung of 7 Feb 2018 — and only as a source for the *remaining* 18–36 LP, never as the 30–42 LP main area. That asymmetry is exactly the kind of rule a validator earns its keep on. On top: a project of ≥9 LP and a seminar must sit inside the chosen Wahlpflicht modules (§5(4)), and §8(2) says up to 30 LP are excluded from the final grade, **of which 12 LP must come from the Wahlbereich**, with the worst-graded modules picked automatically.

By contrast M.Sc. **Computer Engineering** *does* have the three-way split — 18 LP Pflicht (Elektromagnetische Felder, Grundlagen der Statistischen Nachrichtentheorie, Theoretische Grundlagen der Informatik), 54–60 LP Wahlpflicht, 12–18 LP Wahlbereich — plus an A/B group rule and a substitution rule. So the brief's framing fits CE, not Informatik.

**4. "English where the programme is explicitly English-taught (ICT Innovation is)" — every single Fak IV master's programme is English-taught.** Electrical Engineering, Computer Science (Informatik), Computer Engineering, Information Systems Management, Digital Media & Technology, Media Technology, Automotive Systems, Computational Neuroscience: all English (<https://www.tu.berlin/en/eecs/academics-teaching/study-offer/masters-programs>). The CS StuPO itself says *"im englischsprachigen Masterstudiengang Computer Science (Informatik)"* and the programme carries the official Zusatzbezeichnung *"international"* since 2018. **I still wrote three of four drafts in German**, because the recipients are German administrative and student-advisory staff at a German university and the pages are German-first — language choice here should track the staff, not the curriculum. ICT Innovation gets English because the whole EIT Digital consortium operates in English. Flagging it so you can flip any of them deliberately rather than by accident.

---

**Hook:**

*"The Anrechnungsbereich is chosen one module at a time and is hard to change later."*

TU Berlin students commit, at each exam registration, which Studiengangsbereich that module counts towards — and TU Berlin's own tutorial tells them changing it afterwards is a favour to ask, not a right. Nothing shows them the running total against §5's 30–42 / 18–36 split, the ≥9 LP project, the seminar, or the 24–30 LP Wahlbereich band until it is too late to fix cheaply. MTS already stores the Wahlregeln; it just never runs them against a student's plan.

Secondary hook for the student advisers: Anlage 2 of the StuPO ends with *"Bei der Erstellung eines individuellen Studienverlaufsplans ist die Studienfachberatung behilflich."* The regulations name them, by name, as the people who do this by hand.

Never say MOSES/MTS is deficient. Say: MTS models the rules for the catalogue, StudyPlanner runs them against one student's draft, and hands it back before the first registration.

## Incumbent tooling / approval process

**Incumbent tooling / approval process:**

MIMUW runs its **own faculty instance of USOSweb** (`usosweb.mimuw.edu.pl`), separate from the central `usosweb.uw.edu.pl`. There is no in-house study-plan validator. The approval chain is:

1. Each cohort is bound to a fixed **siatka studiów** (course grid) for its whole degree: *"Poniżej znajdziesz siatki zajęć obowiązujące studentów rozpoczynających studia w poszczególnych latach akademickich. Zasada jest prosta: jeśli rozpocząłeś studia w 2019 roku, aż do końca studiów obowiązuje Cię siatka zajęć dla rocznika 2019/20."* — [Plany studiów (czyli co kiedy zaliczyć i podpiąć)](https://www.mimuw.edu.pl/pl/informator-dla-studentow/plany-studiow-czyli-co-kiedy-zaliczyc-i-podpiac/)
2. Student registers for courses themselves in USOSweb (from year 2 of MSc, for everything except the seminar).
3. After the exam session the student does **podpięcia** — manually attaching each passed course to the correct etap — and then submits the programme for **rozliczenie etapu**.
4. The **dziekanat / Sekcja Studencka** performs the actual settlement; deviations and individual arrangements go to the **prodziekan ds. studenckich** (dr Marcin Engel, prof. ucz.) as *podania* in USOSweb.

**What USOS actually validates — the answer, and it generalises across Polish universities:**

USOS validation is **retrospective and arithmetic, not prospective and structural.** Three separate levels, none of which is plan validation:

- **At registration time (rejestracja / rejestracja żetonowa):** USOSweb enforces only *registration-level* constraints — group capacity limits, registration rounds (tury), token balances (żetony) for OGUN/PE/language courses, and **dedykacje** (a group can be restricted to students of a given programme+stage, which blocks anyone else). It does **not** check whether the resulting set of courses will satisfy the degree. MIMUW says this outright to students: *"Powinieneś tak wybierać przedmioty, aby spełnić wszystkie wymagania etapowe."* — [Rejestracje na przedmioty](https://www.mimuw.edu.pl/pl/informator-dla-studentow/rejestracje-na-przedmioty/). The burden is explicitly on the student.
- **At settlement time (rozliczenie etapu):** USOS *does* have a **wymagania etapowe** mechanism and an automatic settlement engine — but it only runs **after** the session, on courses that are already completed and graded, and only after the student has manually done the podpięcia. It answers "did this year add up?", not "will this plan work?". Final sign-off is a human in the dziekanat. Sources: [MIMUW — Rozliczenie etapu](https://www.mimuw.edu.pl/pl/informator-dla-studentow/rozliczenie-etapu-podpiecia-warunkowy-wpis-powtarzanie-etapu/), [USOS wdrożeniowa dokumentacja](https://www.usos.edu.pl/sites/default/files/pl-usos-dokumentacja-wdrozeniowa_0.pdf) (*"aby miało to sens, należy wcześniej zdefiniować wymagania etapowe"* — i.e. requirement checking is an optional, per-faculty configuration, not a guarantee).
- **What the wymagania etapowe actually encode:** a **pooled ECTS point total per category**, not the composition of the plan. MIMUW documents the resulting distortion themselves, and this is the single best sentence in the whole research: for Informatyka II stopnia 2025/26, *"Ponieważ fundamentalne przedmioty systemowe, fundamentalne przedmioty teoretyczne oraz przedmioty obowiązkowe dla jednych specjalności mogą być jednocześnie przedmiotami obieralnymi dla innych specjalności, **wymagania punktowe w USOS w zakresie punktów za przedmioty obieralne (wyrażone punktami 1000-INF-OBIER) stanowią na I roku sumę punktów za 3 standardowe przedmioty obieralne po 6 ECTS, 2 przedmioty obowiązkowe dla danej specjalizacji oraz wymagany przedmiot fundamentalny**"* — [Informatyka II stopnia rocznik 2025/26](https://www.mimuw.edu.pl/pl/informator-dla-studentow/plany-studiow-czyli-co-kiedy-zaliczyc-i-podpiac/informatyka-ii-stopnia/informatyka-ii-stopnia-rocznik-202526/).

  Read that carefully: because the same course can be compulsory for one specialisation and elective for another, MIMUW **collapsed three structurally distinct requirements into one lump ECTS counter in USOS**. A student can therefore hit the USOS number while having the wrong specialisation composition. USOS cannot detect it; only prose on a web page explains it. **That is the gap, and MIMUW documented it themselves.**

**Bottom line for the campaign:** across USOS-based Polish universities, "does USOS validate my plan before I commit to it?" is **no**. It handles per-course registration and limits at the front end, and a post-hoc arithmetic settlement at the back end. Nothing in between. This finding should generalise to Politechnika Warszawska, UJ, AGH, and every other USOS/MUCI member.

# 6. Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa

Researched 2026-08-27. All emails below were read off official `tecnico.ulisboa.pt` pages. Nothing pattern-guessed.

---

**Incumbent tooling / approval process:**

IST is the origin of **Fénix / FenixEdu** — they wrote it and open-sourced it (github.com/FenixEdu/fenixedu-academic). This is not a generic SIS they bought. And the evidence that it validates curriculum rules is not inference, it is visible on their public pages:

1. **Curricular rules are data in the system, not prose in a PDF.** Every course-curriculum page carries a "Mostrar Regras" / **"Show Rules"** toggle. The MEEC curricular-plan page instructs students literally: *"Cada Área de Especialização está organizada em grupos de disciplinas, em que o número de créditos ECTS/UCs a realizar em cada grupo é definido segundo um conjunto de regras indicadas na página do Currículo do MEEC (clicar no botão "Show Rules" no topo da página, à direita)."*
   — https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/cursos/meec/plano-curricular

2. **The rendered rules are exactly the checks StudyPlanner performs.** The MECD curriculum page publicly renders, per group: `Créditos Requeridos: 6.0` on each of the three feeder groups (EEC / CS / Matemática), `27.0 – 30.0` on tronco comum, `63.0 – 68.0` on opções, `Número de Créditos para Inscrição na Unidade: 60.0` on the dissertation (a hard credits-threshold precondition), and `Inscrição com Aprovação do Coordenador` on free electives (an approval-gated rule).
   — https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/cursos/mecd/curriculo

3. **Enrolment is group-scoped, i.e. Fénix knows which specialisation a course is being counted toward.** MEIC FAQ: *"Aquando da inscrição no Fenix, é preciso ter a certeza de ter escolhido essa UC no agrupamento de disciplinas da especialização Y."*
   — https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/cursos/meic-a/faq

4. **ECTS ceilings are enforced at enrolment.** *"máximo de 36 créditos ECTS por cada semestre"*, up to 42 for finalists.
   — https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/en/education/study-at-tecnico/academic-information/enrolments/

5. **The FenixEdu domain model** ships a `CurricularRule` hierarchy (CreditsLimit, DegreeModulesSelectionLimit, AnyCurricularCourse, composite And/Or/Not rules) that the enrolment process evaluates.
   — https://github.com/FenixEdu/fenixedu-academic

**What Fénix does NOT appear to do (the only real gaps found):**

- **No safe sandbox — enrolment is irreversible.** MEIC FAQ, verbatim: *"Não existe possibilidade de remover inscrições."* Combined with *"Cada especialização está limitada a quatro UCs"*, the student makes an irreversible, group-scoped choice with no undo. Fixing a mistake means a `requerimento` to the Direcção Académica.
- **No forward projection toward the specialisation notation.** MEIC's 14 áreas need ≥24 ECTS each for the diploma notation; MEEC needs 54 ECTS principal + 18 ECTS secondary. These are *completion* conditions. No public evidence Fénix tells a 1st-semester student "on this trajectory you will not reach the notation."
- **Exception paths are off-system and on paper.** MEEC opção livre requires a printed form from the DEEC secretariat (5º piso, Torre Norte), submitted in the first week of classes, signed off by Coordenação — https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/cursos/meec/questoes-frequentes-faq

**Corrections to the brief — four things you got wrong:**

1. **"BEFORE the plan is submitted for approval" does not exist at IST.** There is no study-plan document, no submission, no approval step in the normal case. The student enrols directly in Fénix and Fénix accepts or rejects against the rules. Approval only appears for *exceptions*: opção livre, changing área de especialização, and courses taken at another ULisboa school. This is the single biggest mismatch — the product's core framing has no counterpart here.

2. **ECTS is not a flat 120.** MEIC-A and MECD are both **120.0 – 126.0 ECTS** (a range, because Atividades Extracurriculares I/II at 3 ECTS each can push it over). Quoting "120 ECTS" at an IST coordinator marks you as not having looked.

3. **"Área de especialização" works differently per programme, and not the way the brief assumes.** In **MEIC** it is an *optional diploma notation*, earned by accumulating ≥24 ECTS inside a track's course group across 14 tracks — the student is not enrolled in a track, they assign each UC to a group at enrolment time. In **MEEC** it *is* a formal upfront choice: a **Principal (54 ECTS)** and a **Secondary (18 ECTS)** area, which must be different from each other.

4. **The academic-administration owner for mestrados is Área de Graduação (AG), not Área de Pós-Graduação (APG).** APG explicitly handles only 3rd cycle (doutoramento) and DFA. Sending master's-programme outreach to `posgraduacao@` would have been an obvious own-goal.

Minor: **"Information Systems"** at IST is **MISE — Informação e Sistemas Empresariais**, a fully *online/distance* programme run out of DEI with mandatory 1–2 week in-person intensives. And **none of the four are English-taught programmes**; MEIC is *"lecionado em Português e em Inglês"*, the rest are Portuguese-primary. All drafts below are therefore in Portuguese.
