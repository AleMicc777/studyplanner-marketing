## Tips

- Send emails Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am recipient's local time.
- Keep the email under 100 words. Shorter = more replies.
- Never attach PDFs in the first email. Link to the demo instead.
- If they reply with interest but say "talk to IT" — ask for the IT contact directly.
- If they reply "not interested" — ask "what would need to be true for it to be useful?"
- Log every reply in the tracking spreadsheet immediately.
