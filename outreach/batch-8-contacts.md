## The pattern this batch adds: the deadline that is not the real deadline

Batch 7 sorted universities by *who checks and how late*. Batch 8 finds a sharper version — places
where the published deadline and the moment of actual commitment are different dates.

| University | The gap |
|---|---|
| **UCLouvain** | Compose the PAE by **30 Sept**. Modify it until **30 Oct**. Jury validates by **31 Oct** — and may refuse and require adaptation. The student has one month of believing they are done before anyone confirms it. |
| **Universität Wien** | The choice of *Ausprägungsfach* and the composition of *Wahlmodule* must be approved by the Studienprogrammleitung **before studies begin**, and course registration is blocked until it is. Approval is a precondition, not a review. |
| **Maastricht** | Free electives require Board of Examiners approval, and the Board answers **within eight weeks**. Eight weeks is most of a teaching period. |
| **Tartu** | Elective modules must be chosen by the end of the first semester — but failing to register for courses within **two weeks** of semester start means exmatriculation. Two deadlines, two orders of magnitude apart. |
| **Charles MFF** | *Volitelné* courses are registered through a separate function (`Zápis (vlastní)`) from the study plan itself, and SIS's *kontroly* check what you completed, not what you are about to choose. |
| **Grenoble Alpes** | The *contrat pédagogique* is fixed at the latest one month after teaching starts — after the student has already been sitting in the courses. |
| **Aalborg** | Electives at another institution need Study Board pre-approval, and only a limited subset of the curriculum's listed electives actually runs in a given semester. |
| **NTUA** | Registration happens in person at the Registrar's Office in the first two weeks of each semester. |

**Maastricht's eight weeks is the sharpest artifact in the batch** and the one worth leading with.
