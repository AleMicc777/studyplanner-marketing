## New in this batch: the thesis is now "when does the system look?"

Batch 6 established *what the student is committed to before anyone checks*. Batch 7 sorts the same
question by **who does the checking and how late**:

| University | Who checks, and when |
|---|---|
| **NTNU** | Faculty approves the *utdanningsplan*, but only after the student confirms it — hard stop **15 Sept / 15 Feb**. Capacity-limited courses had to be claimed back on **1 June**. |
| **Groningen** | Student may "compile your own package and submit it to the Board of Examiners". The Board is the *first* validator, and it sits after the choice. |
| **TU Graz** | *Freie Wahlfächer* need not be assigned to a curriculum slot at all — "this is finalized at graduation." The mismatch surfaces years late, at the worst moment. |
| **Padova** | Two plan classes: *Automatically Accepted* (own department only) vs *Requiring approval* (anything outside it). Choosing breadth means choosing a review queue. |
| **Aarhus** | Electives go into a *study contract* approved by the head of degree programme. |
| **FEUP** | Optional units are first-come-first-served with capped seats, and the student must self-verify ECTS and timetable compatibility before clicking. |
| **UPM / UPV** | *Automatrícula*: the student enrols in every course for the year in one sitting, optatividad included. |
| **ČVUT FIT** | Surplus *povinně volitelné* courses silently reclassify as *volitelné* — the totals move under the student. |
| **BME VIK** | Mintatanterv splits into k / kv / sp / szv; 40 credits of the specialisation must come from the sp+kv blocks. |

The strongest single artifact in this batch is **TU Graz**: an institution that openly defers elective
validation to graduation day. That is the product's thesis stated by the university itself.
